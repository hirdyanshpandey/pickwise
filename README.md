# PickWise — zero-budget static affiliate site

A free-hostable educational home-office buying-guide website. No payments, database, inventory or paid APIs are required. Homepage includes ten practical buying checklists and working search/filter. Includes affiliate disclosure, privacy, terms, and a scenario calculator.

## Deploy for free

Use Cloudflare Pages **Direct Upload**: create a free Cloudflare account, go to Workers & Pages > Create > Pages > Drag and drop, and upload the **contents of `pickwise-site`** (or a ZIP containing those files at its root). This requires you to authenticate and approve Cloudflare's terms; do not buy a domain or premium plan.

## Actual affiliate links

No affiliate links are live. Register with an affiliate programme, verify eligibility and bank/tax details, and verify that *website* promotion is allowed. Only then add relevant product guides and approved affiliate links, clearly labelled `Sponsored link` with `rel="sponsored noopener noreferrer"` and `target="_blank"`. Keep current retailer prices off-site unless authorized. `affiliate-links.json` is a placeholder for future CMS/data integration, not a live integration.

## Publishing and quality

Check source credibility and inspect every guide before publication. Do not scrape retailers without permission, fabricate product data or automate posting of low-quality generated articles. Test links and disclosures regularly.

## SEO

Pages have titles and meta descriptions. `robots.txt` permits indexing. Submit a sitemap after the final public domain is known; do not submit invented URLs. This is an offline starter, not a launched online business.

## Costs and earnings

The starter itself costs ₹0, but some affiliate services require eligibility/approval, and commissions may be withheld until validation. December ₹5 lakh is an aggressive ambition and not a forecast.
